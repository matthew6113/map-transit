## Openings found

Nothing new. From the repo (Callwell):
- Metropolitan Railway, October 1892; Mission St electric, 1894-09-15; 18th & Park, November 1894
  (CW p. 16) [repo].
- Park & Ocean electric from June 1898 (CW p. 10) [repo]; Ferries & Cliff House electric from May
  1905 (CW p. 12) [repo].
- 1906: streetcars "on Market to Castro and on Market and Valencia to 28th St." within days of
  1906-05-03 (CW p. 21) [repo]; Sutter St electric a few weeks after May 1906 (CW p. 21) [repo].
- URR incorporated 1902-03-04 (CW p. 19); became MSRy 1921-04-01 (CW p. 35); city took over 5 a.m.
  1944-09-29 (CW p. 44) [repo].

## Street-name equivalences

Unchanged from the repo: Central Ave = Presidio Ave (CW p. 8); H St = Lincoln Way (CW p. 10); Army
St = Cesar Chavez St (CW p. 35); Howard St south of 12th = South Van Ness Ave (CW p. 13); China Ave =
Excelsior Ave (CW p. 16); Stanley Place = Sterling St (CW p. 14); East St = The Embarcadero (CW
p. 8); Montgomery Ave = Columbus Ave (CAM p. 15). **Still unsourced:** 13th St = Duboce Ave; D St =
Fulton St; Point Lobos Ave = Geary Blvd west of Presidio Ave; 1st Ave = Arguello Blvd.
