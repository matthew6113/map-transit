# North Bay street railways: Santa Rosa, Petaluma, San Rafael (1874–1907)

Research pass of Oct 16, 2026. Used for `data/past/north-bay-horsecars/`; its downloads were not kept,
and each entry's archive.org id reopens it.

This pass could open archive.org (US servers: metadata, OCR text, search-inside and **page images**)
and files.cpuc.ca.gov. CDNC, loc.gov/Chronicling America, HathiTrust, Google Books, Calisphere,
davidrumsey.com and every newspaper site were blocked (403), and WebFetch failed DNS. So no
Santa Rosa or Petaluma newspaper was read; the only newspapers reached are the *Los Angeles Times*
and the Sonoma *Index-Tribune* scans on archive.org.

| San Rafael | — | **None built** (see §6) | — | — | Only franchise bids (1905) |

**Motive power:** no North Bay city line was ever electrified as a city line. The only change from
horse to electric is the Petaluma & Santa Rosa's interurban on Fourth St, Santa Rosa (1905), laid as
new standard-gauge track under a new franchise (§5).

**Shared track with the drawn P&SR (`psr-santa-rosa-fourth-st`)**: the 1877 horsecar line ran on the
same stretch of Fourth St (SF & North Pacific depot to McDonald Ave) that the P&SR later used from
Wilson St to McDonald Ave. Same street, but **not the same track**: the horsecars were 3 ft 6 in
gauge, and the P&SR laid its own track under a new 1904 franchise and "never" used the horsecar
franchises (CRC D2348 p. 760 [O][img]; Borden p. 6 [S][img]). Whether horsecars and electric cars
ran side by side on Fourth St in 1905 is unsourced.

## 8. Conflicts

1. **Santa Rosa 1877 line opening:** 1877 (court finding, [O][img]) vs 1878 (Borden [S][img]).
   Recommend year 1877, day unsourced; "about June 7, 1877" is only the city's 1886 allegation.
2. **Union Street Railway rename:** May 8, 1893 (Borden; *SRR* 1893) vs 1889 (McGraw 1906).
3. **Union's purchase of the 1877 line:** Dec 24, 1897 (*LA Times* [img]; Borden's Union paragraph)
   vs Dec 27, 1897 (Borden's other paragraph). Take Dec 24.
4. **Petaluma sale:** sheriff's sale Sept 27, 1897 (*LA Times* [img]) vs "foreclosure sale in 1900"
   (Borden; ASRI 1903; McGraw 1905). Possibly sale 1897, title or deed 1900 — unresolved.
5. **Santa Rosa horsecar end:** 1905 (Borden p. 5 and map) vs "not known" (Borden p. 6) vs about 1907
   (2004 survey, no source). Directories still list horses in Mar 1906 and Feb 1907.
6. **"Built between 1888 and 1891"** (Wikipedia, from *Western Railroader* Feb 1952 [ocr]) is wrong:
   the Fourth St line ran from 1877 and the Central opened in 1892.
7. **Lengths and gauges** (map-irrelevant): South Side 4 ft 8½ in (SRJ 1892) vs 3 ft 6 in (Borden,
   ASRI 1897); Central 3 mi (manuals) vs 3.5 (Borden); Petaluma 2.5 (Borden) vs 2 (manuals) vs 1.81
   (Census 1902). The 1877 line 1.5 (manuals) vs 1.62 (Borden) vs 2 (Whipple 1890 [ocr]).
8. **P&SR incorporation** (out of scope): May 22, 1903 (CRC D2348 [ocr]) vs June 20, 1903 (Borden,
   McGraw).
