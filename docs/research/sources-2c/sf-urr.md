## How sourced (read first)

- **archive.org**: only the OCR text could be read (`https://archive.org/stream/<id>/<id>_djvu.txt`).
  Page images and the `/download/` data hosts are refused by this container's proxy, so **every
  archive.org quote here is [ocr]** and no page image was checked. Printed page numbers are read from
  the page numbers in the OCR text:
  - **OS29** prints its page number at the *top* of each typed page (the number precedes the page's
    text in the OCR; blank versos show as OCR noise). p. 344 has no number in the OCR; it is
    inferred between 343 and 345.
  - **MAN09** prints its page number at the *foot* of each page (the number follows the page's text).
    The page assignment below matches the report's own contents list on p. 3 for every line.
- **OCR misreads of line numbers in OS29.** The typewriter's 3 reads as 5 in the OCR (and 2 or 0
  sometimes as 5/50). The appendix headings read "Line No, 5 – Sutter and Jackson", "Line ITo. 25 –
  Fillmore and Valencia", "Line No. 50 – Eighth and Army", "52 – Hayes and Oak", "55 – Eighteenth
  Street and Park", "54 – Sixth and Sansome", "55 – Howard", "56 – Folsom". Each is corrected below
  only where the report's own text gives the right number elsewhere (cited per line), or where the
  sequence leaves no other reading (flagged "by sequence").
- CPUC files (files.cpuc.ca.gov) download as PDFs; quotes from them are from `pdftotext` and are
  marked [pdf]; when checked on a rendered page image they are marked [img].
- Tags: [P] primary, [O] official, [C] contemporary compiled history, [W] pointer only.

## Key facts about the numbering

- **Numbers were new in January 1909.** MAN09 p. 34 [ocr] [P], special report dated "Jan. 7, 1909":
  "a special examination has been made of the new system of numbering street cars to designate their
  routes". It adds: "there are no numbers 2, 13, 17. Numbers 10, 11, 12, 14, 15, 16 have not been
  placed on the respective cars as yet."
- MAN09 p. 33 [ocr]: the United Railroad "has adopted a system of numbering the lines, placing a
  number about eighteen inches high on the front of the roof of each car."
- MAN09 p. 8 [ocr], "NUMBERED LINES OF THE UNITED RAILROADS. Table showing number of each of the
  street car lines on February 1, 1909": 1 Sutter; 3 Sutter and Jackson; 4 Turk and Eddy;
  5 McAllister; 6 Masonic; 7 Haight; 8 Market; 9 Valencia; 10 Guerrero, and (also 10) Glen Park;
  11 Mission and 24th; 12 Ingleside; 14 Cemeteries; 15 3rd and Kearny; 16 Kentucky; 18 Mission and
  Polk; 19 Bryant and Polk; 20 Ellis and Ocean; 21 Hayes and Ellis; 22 Fillmore; 23 Fillmore and
  Mission; 24 Mission and Chutes.
- MAN09 p. 34 [ocr]: two lines shared No. 15 ("These lines were numbers 15 and 17, but 17 was
  consolidated with 15") and two shared No. 10.
- In 1909 many URR lines had **no number**: Bryant, Folsom, Howard, Eighteenth, Montgomery and 10th,
  6th and Sansome, 8th and 18th, Mail Dock, Mission and Brannan, San Bruno, Railroad Avenue, the
  extension lines, the cable lines and the San Mateo line (MAN09 pp. 19–30). Their 1929 numbers are
  given below where the 1909 route plainly matches; that match is an inference, flagged each time.
- OS29 (May 1929) lists MSRy "Main and Crosstown trolley Routes" Nos. 1–12, 14–30, 32–36, 40–42
  (no 13, 31, 37–39), then unnumbered "Extension, Shuttle and Short Line Electric Routes", cable
  routes and motor coach routes (OS29 pp. 343–352). OS29 p. 343 [ocr]: "Except where otherwise
  noted, cars of each line operate in both direct ions between terminals over the route described."

## Line by line

Format: route lists are in **today's street names**, in running order, as `Street: from → to`; a
name change is marked ⟨old name⟩ and cited in "Street-name equivalences" at the end. "Ferry" =
the Ferry Building loop at the foot of Market St; "Ferry Terminal via Embarcadero" = from the Ferry
Building along The Embarcadero to the named street. End dates: see each line; *pending* = not yet
found in this pass.

### No. 1 Sutter (1909) / Sutter and California Streets (1929)

- Operator: URR (1909), MSRy (1929).
- **1909-02-01** (MAN09 p. 13 [ocr] [P]): "From Market and Sutter out Sutter to Presidio Ave. to
  California to 6th Ave., every other car going to the beach via the old Cliff House Steam Line."
  Two horsecars then ran "on the outside tracks on Market Street from Sutter and Market to the
  Ferry" (MAN09 pp. 13–14).
  - Sutter St: Market → Presidio Ave; Presidio Ave: Sutter → California; California St: Presidio
    Ave → 6th Ave. Alternate cars on to the beach over the former Ferries & Cliff House line
    (its streets are not given here; see the repo's CW p. 12 entry).
- **1929** (OS29 p. 343 [ocr] [O]): "From Ferry Loop via Market Street, Sutter Street, Presidio
  Avenue, California Street, Sixth Avenue and Clement Street to Thirty-third Avenue. Owl service is
  operated' every sixty minutes".
  - Market St: Ferry → Sutter; Sutter St: Market → Presidio Ave; Presidio Ave: Sutter → California;
    California St: Presidio Ave → 6th Ave; 6th Ave: California → Clement; Clement St: 6th Ave →
    33rd Ave.
- End of streetcar service: *pending*.
