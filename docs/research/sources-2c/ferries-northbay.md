### Terminals (exact)

- **San Francisco:** "the vehicle slips near the foot of Mission Street" (D29338 p. 1), which the
  same decision and D29999 call the "Ferry Building Terminal"; D29464 lists the landing as
  "Mission Street". So: the SP vehicle slips at the foot of Mission St, just south of the Ferry
  Building, treated by the Commission as part of the Ferry Building terminal.
- **Oakland:** "a slip owned by applicant on Oakland Estuary near the foot of Broadway" = "Broadway
  Wharf"; terminal grounds at "1st and Broadway" (D29338 pp. 1, 5).
- **1876:** Halley gives only "the new wharf at the foot of Broadway"; the San Francisco landing in
  1876 is not stated on p. 435 (unsourced).

### Conflicts

- **The SF terminal name:** "Ferry Building Terminal" (D29338, D29999) vs "Mission Street" (D29464)
  — resolved: D29338 p. 1 names both in one decision (Ferry Building Terminal = the vehicle slips
  near the foot of Mission St).
- None on the dates: the re-opening (Halley) and the order (D29999) each have one source.

### For Matthew

- Decision 17: close **on or about 1937-08**, citing D29999 (order dated 1937-08-09). Note for the
  panel: the order let the company cancel its schedules on 5 days' notice any time within 30 days,
  so the real last run fell between about 1937-08-14 and 1937-09-08; it may be September.
- Newspapers that would date the last boat: Oakland Tribune, Aug 10 – Sept 10, 1937 (archive.org
  `oakland-tribune-1937-08-…` if present; not searched yet).
- **Bonus — Hyde St–Berkeley (also D29999):** CRC D30707 (A21297, 1938-03-21) [O][img], p. 2: "The
  period of suspension expired February 15, 1938." A six-month suspension ending Feb 15, 1938 means
  the boats stopped about **Aug 15–16, 1937**. The order (p. 5) granted abandonment, "together with
  authority to remove or dispose of terminal facilities", and directed the company "to cancel

  "The effective date of this order shall be the date hereof", dated "21st day of March, 1938"
  [img]. So Berkeley: service suspended from about 1937-08-16 (derived), abandoned 1938-03-21.
  File: …/Decisions_D25901_to_D40599/D30707_19380321_A21297.pdf.
