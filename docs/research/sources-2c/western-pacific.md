# Research: Western Pacific passenger trains in the Bay Area (1910–1970), and the 1862 Western Pacific's San José – Vallejo's Mills line

Gathered 2026-10-16. Extends `railroads.md` §2 (the 1862 WP, the CP's 1869 openings) and
`ferries-northbay.md` §2b (the end of the Oakland Pier ferry, CPUC D57169). Bay Area portion
only: the 1903 company's line from its Oakland terminals to the San Joaquin county line, and its
1922 San José branch. The line crosses the county line between Midway (MP 63, in Alameda County
per [W]) and Carbona (MP 72, renamed "Tracy" by 1970); the exact point is not in these sources.

## How this pass worked, and how far to trust it

- **archive.org page images worked** (`<server>.us.archive.org/BookReader/BookReaderImages.php`).
  Every key date and route fact below was read on the page image **[img]**; [ocr] marks the rest.
  Leaves are given as `n<i>` (BookReader index); a leaf link is
  `https://archive.org/details/<id>/page/n<i>/`.
- **Sources found this pass:**
  - The *Oakland Tribune* for 1874–1924 is on archive.org (`oakland-tribune-YYYY-MM-DD`,
    collection `newspaperarchive-oakland-tribune`; public domain, pre-1929).
  - 107 **WP employee timetables** (CSRM, `cscrm_2021_02_*`, 1919–1970). The Western Division
    ones (Oakland – Stockton) are dated 1919 and 1932–1970. They are "copyright status unknown":
    facts only.
  - SP Western Division employee timetables, from the same CSRM set.
  - Later California papers in `newspaperarchive`: *San Rafael Daily Independent-Journal*
    1951–75, *Red Bluff Daily News*, Long Beach *Independent Press-Telegram* and *Lompoc
    Record*. These are 1958–70 issues, probably still in copyright: facts only, short quotes.
  - The Niles *Township Register* 1927–54 (`cafrwtm_*`) and the *Point Richmond Progress*
    (`cric_*`); both copyright unknown.
- **Commission decisions** (files.cpuc.ca.gov) were rendered with `pdftoppm` and read on the
  image. A bulk scan read pp. 1–2 of all 2,658 CPUC decisions dated 1959-10-01 → 1961-05-01. It
  looked for the Zephyrette's end and found none (see §1g). The earlier passes' scan (pp. 1–3,
  1916–18 and 1923–37) was grepped for WP passenger and ferry matters.
- Blocked or not tried: CDNC, HathiTrust, Google Books (403), so no ICC reports were opened. The
  *Oakland Tribune* after 1924 isn't on archive.org.
- **Tags:** [O] official (commission order, sworn statement in a federal court record); [P]
  primary (railroad timetable, notice or advertisement, contemporary newspaper); [C] contemporary
  compiled (directories, county histories, trade journals, the Railroad Commissioners' reports);
  [S] later secondary; [W] pointer only; [img] read on the page image; [ocr] text layer only.

**Answer (the coordinator's question):**

- WP's own ferry, from its mole to the Ferry Building, ran **1910-08-22 → 1918**.
- **First stint at the SP's Oakland Pier:** from the USRA terminal consolidation of 1918 (a proposal on 05-28;
  the WP station disused by 10-25) to the end of federal control, 1920.
- WP then **returned to its own mole**; the exact day of the return is unsourced, and the end of federal
  control is 1920-03-01 [W].
- **WP trains last used their own mole between 1933-06-09 and 1933-10-01.** They were still at
  the mole in the WP timetable of 1933-05-10; the move was still to come on 06-09; and the SP's
  timetable of 10-01 carries them. The day isn't in any source read.
- **Second stint at the SP's Oakland Pier:** 1933 → **1958-07-29**, with the last ferry that
  night. From 1958-07-30, WP passenger trains ended at Oakland, with buses to SF (CZ only).

- **Built:** yes. The company swore on **1866-09-29** that the San José – Alameda Cañon 20 miles

- **Against:**

- **The map should treat 1866–69 operation as possible but undated:**

## Method notes

- **Newspapers by date:** `https://archive.org/services/search/v1/scrape?q=collection:newspaperarchive AND coverage:(California)`
  lists every California title and year. Search one paper with the full-text endpoint and
  `page_target=newspaperarchive-oakland-tribune`. `fulltext/inside.php` needs `doc=` set to the
  stem of the `_djvu.txt` file (`<id>_access` for CSRM items), not the item id.
- **The terminal question was settled from the timetables:** count "Western Pacific Mole" against
  "Oakland Pier" in each Western Division timetable's text, then read the dated pages on the image.
- The pass's downloads (texts, page images, decision scans) were not kept; each entry's archive.org id
  or CPUC file reopens it.
