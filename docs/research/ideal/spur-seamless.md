Oct 16, 2026. Research only, for the reference layer (`ideal/reference/<proposal>.geojson`,
`docs/SCHEMA.md` "Ideal network"). It edits no data and recommends nothing: the ideal network is
Matthew's to design. Link21, ConnectSF, the Muni Subway Vision, Dumbarton rail, a Geary/19th Avenue
subway and regional express rail are covered from the agencies' own documents by another researcher;
this file only notes where SPUR or Seamless Bay Area names those corridors.

## How the pass worked, and its limits

- **No page was opened.** From this container spur.org, seamlessbayarea.org and web.archive.org
  are refused, and WebFetch fails. Every fact below comes from the WebSearch tool, with
  `allowed_domains` set to `spur.org` or `seamlessbayarea.org` (standard mode, then extended).
- **Every fact is `[srch]`:** search-result text, not a reading of the page. "Quoted words" are the
  strings the tool's summary presented as the page's own words; boundaries aren't always marked, so a
  quote may be lightly trimmed. Which URL a quote came from is the tool's attribution. Everything
  else is *(paraphrase)*.
- **Dates** are the documents' own (from the page, the PDF's cover text or the URL's date path),
  not when the search indexed them. "Undated" means none was seen.
- **The search budget ran out partway** (a 200-call limit shared by every agent this turn). Not
  searched: SPUR on Dumbarton rail; the list of nine ideas in SPUR's 2021 San Francisco report; the
  lines on Seamless Bay Area's Vision Map; anything from MTC, Link21, SFMTA or SamTrans confirming
  that an agency took up a piece. The leads are listed at the end.
- **No agency source was seen,** so no piece below is marked `adopted` or `study` on this file's
  evidence alone. Where SPUR or Seamless Bay Area *says* an agency is studying something, that is
  noted as their claim, to check against the agency's own documents.
